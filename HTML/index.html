How the decision is made

    
The model estimates each customer’s chance of leaving. If that chance is at or above the cutoff (), we recommend a retention offer.

    
Why the cutoff is low: losing a customer costs about $500, while an unneeded offer costs about $50. Since missing a churner is roughly 10× worse, the cutoff is lowered from 0.50 to favour catching more churners.

    
Model: outlier clipping, feature engineering, preprocessing, and a calibrated XGBoost classifier. Missing Tenure or distance values are filled with the training medians.

    
Test results: ROC-AUC 0.9596. Recall 92.52% at the cutoff (99 of 107 churners found). Estimated test-set cost fell from $12,500 to $7,950.
